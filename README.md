# 📊 Marketing Performance Optimization Analysis

Marketing Performance Optimization for a Growing E-Commerce Company.
The company runs monthly marketing campaigns using:
● Facebook Ads
● Google Ads
● Influencer promotions
● Seasonal discount campaigns


---

## 🧠 Project Overview

This project analyzes 10 months of marketing data (January–October 2024)
to evaluate campaign efficiency, calculate Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC),
detect anomalies, and provide strategic business recommendations.

| `Executive_Summary.docx` | Final business insight report |
